# Air Protocols Offline iPhone/iPad Test Checklist

Use a production Vercel deployment and a physical iPhone or iPad. Complete the download while connected to reliable Wi-Fi.

1. Deploy the committed application to Vercel.
2. Open the Vercel site in Safari.
3. Tap Share, choose **Add to Home Screen**, and confirm installation.
4. Launch Air Protocols from the Home Screen and open **Settings**.
5. In **Offline Access**, tap **Download for Offline Use**.
6. Keep the app open until the status reads **Ready for Offline Use**. Tap **Verify Offline Content** and confirm it remains ready.
7. Enable airplane mode and confirm both Wi-Fi and cellular data are unavailable.
8. Completely close Air Protocols from the app switcher.
9. Reopen Air Protocols from its Home Screen icon.
10. Open several protocols that were never viewed before the download, including at least one Reference protocol.
11. Open **Operations Manual** and then **Open Full Manual**.
12. Navigate forward and backward between several Operations Manual PDF pages and use direct page entry.
13. Add and remove a favorite, confirm Recently Viewed updates, and search for protocols from multiple categories.
14. Open **Settings** and confirm the offline package still reports **Ready for Offline Use**.
15. Disable airplane mode, deploy a package with a newer app or protocol version, reopen Settings, and confirm **Update Available** appears while the old package remains usable.
